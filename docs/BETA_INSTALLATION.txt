BYE Apple AI Beta — @BETA_VERSION@

1. Open the DMG.
2. Drag BYE Apple AI Beta into Applications.
3. Eject the DMG and open BYE Apple AI Beta from Applications.

This beta installs beside the previous BYE app. Its saved profiles, choices and
summaries are stored separately in ~/Library/Application Support/BYEAppleAIBeta.
The package excludes pre-existing caches and preferences.

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac; macOS 14+ for ratings/reviews. Apple summaries
require macOS 26.4+, Apple Intelligence enabled, and the system model ready.
No Ollama, API key or separate AI application is needed. macOS may need to
download Apple's model once. Summaries run on device; PCC/RMP retrieval needs
internet. This beta has no cloud AI or automatic Ollama fallback.

This is a locally signed early-tester beta, not an Apple-notarized public release.
If macOS blocks opening a copy you received, follow Apple's instructions for
opening a trusted app in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Only approve
the copy you trust; do not disable Gatekeeper globally. Managed Macs may not
permit this approval.
https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unknown-developer-mh40616/mac

Testing: search a PCC course/semester, open a professor, check the AI summary
against the original reviews, then quit/reopen to test cached results.
The summary states how many downloaded reviews it analyzed; it may use a
bounded recent sample to fit Apple's model. Ratings are professor-wide.
Multiple eligible profiles require your explicit choice. A model error never
replaces source ratings or reviews with invented data.

Known limits: summary latency varies. First-use model loading, review retrieval
and longer input can increase waiting.
The RMP API is unofficial and may change or reject requests. Apple summaries are experimental. Conservative source checks can omit valid
themes or show that no theme could be verified. Retry summary reuses downloaded
reviews. AI paraphrases can still be wrong; original source links remain available. Windows and Intel
distribution are not included in this beta.

Credits/license notices: inside the app at
Contents/Resources/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

Updating: quit the existing Apple AI beta, drag the new beta into Applications,
and replace only BYE Apple AI Beta when prompted. Its local data is retained.
To reopen a closed window, click the app in the Dock or open it from Applications.
