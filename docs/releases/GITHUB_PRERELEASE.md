# BYE Beta 0.5.0

BYE is back. Find PCC classes that fit your week, check open seats, and compare professor ratings with reviews from PCC students and Rate My Professors.

**Download `BYE-0.5.0-AppleSilicon.dmg` from this release’s Assets.** The `BYE-0.5.0-AppleSilicon.zip` file is the in-app update package; you do not need to download it. The source-code ZIP and tar.gz files are for developers, not installers. The `.sha256` assets contain SHA-256 checksums.

## What changed

- **BYE is back on.** Class searches, professor ratings, reviews and update checks work again after 0.4.1 turned them off.
- **Reviews from PCC students.** Professor reports now show BYE reviews from PCC students who verified their school email, alongside Rate My Professors reviews. BYE reviews load first.
- **Your own BYE number (optional).** Verify your PCC school email to get a student number like BYE-0042 on your student card. It stays yours on any Mac. Skip it if you like; your name and classes stay on this Mac.
- **Fresher seat counts.** Schedules and open seats come from BYE’s schedule service, with a “Checked N min ago” note. Older schedules hide seat counts instead of showing stale ones.
- **A friendlier start.** A short welcome asks what to call you, and the command menu (Command K) jumps to any class or setting.
- **Your classes.** Remove a class from Your classes with one click.
- BYE still never ranks professors; you compare and decide.

## Install or update

1. Quit any running BYE beta.
2. Open the DMG and drag **BYE Beta** into **Applications**.
3. Eject the DMG and open BYE Beta from Applications.
4. If upgrading from **BYE Apple AI Beta**, confirm BYE Beta works, then remove the old app to avoid launching the previous version. Existing beta data is reused; deleting the old app bundle does not delete that data. The original BYE app remains separate.

Coming from **0.4.1**, beta.4 or earlier? Install this one by hand once, because 0.4.1 no longer checks for updates. After that, future versions install through the app; keep BYE Beta in Applications so it can replace itself. If you have 0.3.0-beta.5 or 0.4.0-beta.1, BYE offers this update on its own.

This beta is locally signed and **not Apple-notarized**. If macOS blocks it and you trust this download, follow [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unknown-developer-mh40616/mac) to approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Managed Macs may not permit approval.

## Requirements and privacy

- Apple Silicon Mac (M-series), macOS 14 or later.
- Internet for class schedules, ratings and reviews. Schedules come from BYE’s schedule service, which copies PCC’s public schedule. BYE reviews and BYE numbers use the BYE website; getting a number sends your PCC school email to BYE to verify it.
- Update checks contact the BYE website about once a day; its host, Cloudflare, sees your public IP address and app version. No system profile or other information is sent. Turn off automatic checks in Settings. Apple Intelligence, Ollama, an account, and an API key are not required.
- No AI model weights are bundled. Saved reviews, choices, and older summaries remain local; older summaries are not displayed or regenerated by this release.
- RMP uses an unofficial endpoint and may fail or change. Requests avoid RMP webpage trackers, persistent cookies, and login state. RMP still sees your public IP and requested queries/profiles. External source links open separately in your browser.
