# BYE Beta 0.4.0-beta.1

Find PCC classes that fit your available days and compare professor ratings and original student reviews.

**Download `BYE-0.4.0-beta.1-AppleSilicon.dmg` from this release’s Assets.** The `BYE-0.4.0-beta.1-AppleSilicon.zip` file is the in-app update package; you do not need to download it. The source-code ZIP and tar.gz files are for developers, not installers. The `.sha256` assets contain SHA-256 checksums.

## What changed

- **A new look.** BYE is redesigned around a warm ivory page, black type and buttons, and one yellow highlight. Headlines and scores use Libre Baskerville, and the app has a new wordmark icon.
- **More about each class.** Class cards now show the format (in person, hybrid, online live or online async), the term (full term or 8-week sessions), the campus, units, and open seats with the waitlist, all from PCC’s schedule. Full sections are marked.
- **Clearer filters.** Pick class days with one-tap day buttons: BYE shows classes that meet on every day you pick (picking Monday shows all Monday classes; Monday and Tuesday shows classes that meet on both). Fully online classes still show. Turn on “Fully online only” from the search screen or the results sidebar.
- **Results open on a course overview.** Instead of jumping straight to one professor, BYE shows the class at a glance (sections, professors, open seats, formats and terms) and lets you pick who to look at.
- **Reviews open on something useful.** When none of a professor’s downloaded reviews mention the class you searched, the report shows reviews from all their courses, with a one-tap way to look for reviews of your class.
- Rating bars and the progress bar while ratings load are shown in yellow. BYE doesn’t label any professor as the best pick; you compare and decide.
- Fixes: new course numbers like STAT C1000 no longer show as "STATC 1000"; hybrid meeting times read clearly ("Online, no set meeting time", "Time TBA · R 218", "room TBA") instead of PCC's raw building codes; review text shows punctuation like — and & instead of codes such as `&#8212;`; course suggestions no longer push the Find button off-screen.
- **In-app updates.** BYE Beta now checks for new versions about once a day and installs them when you quit. Every update is signature-checked before it installs. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings or use **Check for Updates…** in the app menu. (This shipped in 0.3.0-beta.5 on the BYE website and is new on GitHub.)
- AI summaries stay paused, as in beta.4.

## Install or update

1. Quit any running BYE beta.
2. Open the DMG and drag **BYE Beta** into **Applications**.
3. Eject the DMG and open BYE Beta from Applications.
4. If upgrading from **BYE Apple AI Beta**, confirm BYE Beta works, then remove the old app to avoid launching the previous version. Existing beta data is reused; deleting the old app bundle does not delete that data. The original BYE app remains separate.

Coming from beta.4 or earlier? Install this one by hand once. After that, future versions install through the app; keep BYE Beta in Applications so it can replace itself. If you already have 0.3.0-beta.5, BYE offers this update on its own.

This beta is locally signed and **not Apple-notarized**. If macOS blocks it and you trust this download, follow [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unknown-developer-mh40616/mac) to approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Managed Macs may not permit approval.

## Requirements and privacy

- Apple Silicon Mac (M-series), macOS 14 or later.
- Internet for PCC/RMP data.
- Update checks contact the BYE website about once a day; its host, Cloudflare, sees your public IP address and app version. No system profile or other information is sent. Turn off automatic checks in Settings. Apple Intelligence, Ollama, an account, and an API key are not required.
- No AI model weights are bundled. Saved reviews, choices, and older summaries remain local; older summaries are not displayed or regenerated by this release.
- RMP uses an unofficial endpoint and may fail or change. Requests avoid RMP webpage trackers, persistent cookies, and login state. RMP still sees your public IP and requested queries/profiles. External source links open separately in your browser.
